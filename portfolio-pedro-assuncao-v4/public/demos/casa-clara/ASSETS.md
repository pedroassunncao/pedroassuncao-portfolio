# Ambientes ilustrativos — Casa Clara

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (modo padrão da habilidade imagegen). São espaços fictícios para uma demonstração de site, não fotos de obras executadas. Os arquivos originais foram preservados; cópias fazem parte deste projeto e são entregues em tamanhos otimizados por `next/image`.

## Arquivos

- `public/demos/casa-clara/estar.png`: imagem principal e estudo de sala.
- `public/demos/casa-clara/cozinha.png`: estudo de cozinha.
- `public/demos/casa-clara/quarto.png`: estudo de quarto.

As capturas `public/projects/casa-desktop.png`, `casa-mobile.png` e `casa-gallery.png` mostram a demonstração implementada; não são mockups gerados.

## Prompts utilizados

### Sala de estar

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape hero photograph for a conceptual Brazilian interiors studio website. Create a photorealistic editorial interior photograph of a calm contemporary Brazilian living room with tall open windows overlooking tropical foliage, a low cream linen sofa, warm oak cabinetry, travertine coffee table and a single terracotta ceramic vase. Soft afternoon daylight, subtle real linen creases and wood grain, understated lived-in warmth. Wide horizontal composition, architectural photography at eye level, straight verticals, no fisheye. Natural warm ivory, olive green, timber and limestone colors. The web page copy will be outside this image. No people, no lettering, no brand marks, no watermarks, no collage. This is an illustrative fictional space, not a real commissioned work.

### Cozinha

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape project-gallery photograph for a conceptual interiors studio website. Photorealistic editorial architecture photography of a small beautifully planned kitchen in a Brazilian apartment, warm oak lower cabinets, muted olive upper cabinets, light quartz backsplash, one generous window, a small breakfast surface with two oak stools. Refined but practical, soft natural morning light, believable cabinet joinery, real wood and stone texture. Eye-level wide horizontal view with straight verticals. Calm warm cream, timber, muted olive palette. No people, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no collage. Fictional illustrative interior, not a commissioned work.

### Quarto

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape project-gallery photograph for a fictional Brazilian interiors studio. Photorealistic editorial interior photograph of a restful contemporary bedroom with a linen-covered bed, warm oak wall paneling, a muted olive bedside table, cream plaster walls, sheer curtains with soft morning sunlight and a textured natural-fiber rug. Comfortable attainable residential design, thoughtful storage and restrained details, realistic fabric wrinkles, wood grain, straight architectural lines. Eye-level wide horizontal composition, natural warm tones. No people, no writing, no brand logos, no watermark, no collage. Fictional illustrative interior, not a real commissioned project.
